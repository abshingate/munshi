First time on this computer

Step 1: A Tally installation window opens by itself — click Install, keep clicking Next. When it asks where to keep DATA, choose C:\TallyData.
Step 2: Open Tally and activate your license (serial number + Tally.net login).
That's it — everything else is already installed.

Daily use

  • Your data: keep every Tally company inside C:\TallyData. It is backed up automatically every night at 2 AM (last 14 nights kept).
  • Switching off: just close your browser tab or click Stop from where you started the machine. This computer also switches itself off after about an hour of inactivity. Nothing is ever lost.
  • Government portals: open the Govt Portals folder on the desktop — GST, Income Tax, TRACES (TDS), EPFO (PF), ESIC, MCA.

Signing with your DSC (USB token)

The token stays plugged into your own computer at home and reaches this computer over the internet:

You connect fromHow to use the DSC
Windows laptopUse the Remote Desktop app. Before connecting, open its settings → Local Resources → tick Smart cards. Done — portals see your token.
Mac / browserUse VirtualHere (in the DSC Setup folder; free for one device). Server part runs on your Mac, the client here. One-time setup.
No DSC at allGST and Income-tax accept an OTP on your Aadhaar-linked mobile — allowed for proprietorships and partnerships. Companies/LLPs must use a DSC.

First time only: install your token's driver from the DSC Setup folder (ePass2003 / ProxKey / mToken — pick your brand). Each portal's signing tool (GST emSigner, TRACES WebSigner, MCA emBridge) can only be downloaded after you log in to that portal with your own account — one double-click, first use only. The 32-bit Java they need is already installed.

Common questions

Will I get a huge bill if I forget to switch off?
No. It stops itself after ~1 hour idle and emails the owner. A monthly budget alarm warns long before spending becomes a problem.

Is my data safe when the computer is off?
Yes — the disk is permanent and encrypted, and backed up nightly. Off only means "not paying for the hours".

Can I use this from my phone or another computer?
Yes, from anywhere — you only need the current address and the password.

Who do I call?
Whoever set this up for you. Show them the Check System Health result — it tells them exactly what's wrong. This system is open source: github.com/abshingate/tally-cloud-workstation
